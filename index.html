06 / FAQs

Questions, answered.

These answers describe the current pre-launch position, not an offer to provide regulated services.

Is Invesec accepting deposits now?

No. This website is an information and expression-of-interest page only. Do not send money to Invesec or any individual claiming to represent it.

Are the $500, $1,000 and $5,000 tiers active?

No. They are proposed account-size examples for planning and discussion only. They are not active investment products.

How does the proposed 70/30 split work?

The preliminary concept is 70% of eligible realized net profits to the client and 30% to the firm. The definition of net profit, high-water mark, loss carry-forward, fees, timing and legal permissibility must be settled in approved written documents before any launch.

Can you guarantee profit or protect my capital?

No. Trading can result in partial or total loss. No profit, return, recovery or capital protection is guaranteed.

Who controls the trading account and funds?

Custody and account-control arrangements have not been approved. Do not share broker passwords, one-time codes, API secrets, wallet seed phrases or banking credentials.

Is Invesec licensed or approved?

This page makes no claim of regulatory approval or licensing. The proposed account-management and profit-sharing model must receive appropriate Nigerian legal and regulatory clearance before it is offered.
